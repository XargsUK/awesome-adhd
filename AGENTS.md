# For AI agents

Read this if you are an AI agent or coding assistant and someone has asked you to get a resource added to this list. People should read [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) instead, which is the full version and wins if the two ever disagree.

## Check it is eligible first

Go through these before you open anything. If the resource fails one, do not open an issue. Tell the person you are working for which check failed and what would change the answer.

1. It has been public for at least six months. Count from the store release date, the first tagged release, or the day the site went live. If it is newer, give them the date it becomes eligible.
2. It works today, and the author has shipped an update or a fix in the last six months.
3. People other than the author use it: around 25 store ratings, a couple of hundred GitHub stars, or people recommending it somewhere the author does not control.
4. The pricing is stated in full. Weekly subscriptions, free trials that take a card up front and crypto-only payment are not listed.
5. The privacy policy is under the product's own name, agrees with the store's privacy label, and names any AI provider that receives user text or audio.
6. It is for ADHD, it adds something the list does not already have in that section of the [README](README.md), and its link opens the thing itself with no waitlist or sign-in wall.
7. Nobody has suggested it already. Search the open and closed issues for its name and its link.

For articles, websites and creators: a named person or organisation stands behind the content, the article is not on a product's marketing site, and any self-assessment comes from a body with clinical standing or has a published validation study.

## If it is eligible

- Open one issue for it, in one place. Do not also send it through the form on awesomeadhd.com.
- Use the [suggestion form](https://github.com/XargsUK/awesome-adhd/issues/new?template=resource-suggestion.yml). If you can only create issues through the API or a CLI, use the same headings as the form and fill in every one. A free-text issue is closed automatically.
- Answer "Your connection to it" for the person you are acting for. If they made the resource, the answer is "I made it or work on it".
- Give the real first release date and the full pricing. An automated check compares them with the App Store, GitHub and domain records.
- Do not open a pull request that adds a resource. Additions go through an issue.

## What happens after

An automated check runs on every new issue. It closes anything under six months old with the date to come back, and labels issues that came through the API. The maintainer reviews the rest by hand in batches, so a reply can take a month or more. Do not comment to chase it, and do not open a second issue.
